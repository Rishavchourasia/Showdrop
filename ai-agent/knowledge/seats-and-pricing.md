# Seats and pricing

## Seat categories
Every show has 6 rows of 10 seats, labelled A to F.
- REGULAR seats are rows A, B, C and D, closest to the screen or stage. Price: Rs. 200 per seat.
- PREMIUM seats are rows E and F, at the back, with the best overall view and more legroom. Price: Rs. 350 per seat.

## Which seats are best?
For movies, the middle of rows D, E or F gives the most comfortable viewing angle. Row A is very close to the screen.
For concerts, stand-up comedy and theatre, rows B to D put you closer to the performers.
Seats 5 and 6 in each row are next to the central aisle, which makes it easy to get in and out.

## Sitting together
Seats with consecutive numbers in the same row are next to each other, for example C4 and C5. Seat 5 and seat 6 are on opposite sides of the aisle.

## Fees and taxes
The price shown on the seat map is the final price. There are no extra booking or convenience fees on Showdrop.

## Group bookings
You can book up to 10 seats in a single booking. For larger groups, make more than one booking.
