# How booking works on Showdrop

## Step by step
1. Pick an event from the home page, then pick a show (a venue, city, date and time).
2. Choose your seats on the seat map. You can select up to 10 seats in one booking.
3. Click Pay. Your seats are now held for you for 5 minutes while you complete payment.
4. On the checkout page, click Pay to confirm. Your booking becomes CONFIRMED and your ticket is sent to your email.

## The 5-minute seat hold
When you start checkout, the seats are reserved only for you for 5 minutes. Nobody else can select them during that time; other customers see them as "held".
If you do not pay within 5 minutes, the hold expires automatically and the seats become available again for everyone. Your booking is then marked EXPIRED.
You can also cancel during checkout with "Cancel & release seats" to free the seats immediately.

## Booking statuses
- PENDING: seats are held, waiting for payment (at most 5 minutes).
- CONFIRMED: paid. The seats are yours.
- CANCELLED: you cancelled during checkout.
- EXPIRED: the 5-minute hold ran out before payment.

## Why did my seat disappear?
If a seat you wanted turns grey (booked) or yellow (held), another customer reached it first. Pick different seats or try again in a few minutes, because held seats are released if that customer does not pay.

## Do I need an account?
You can browse events and seat maps without an account. To book, you must sign up or log in. Your bookings are listed under "My bookings".
