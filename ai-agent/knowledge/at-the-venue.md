# At the venue

## Entry
Show your booking (the confirmation email or the "My bookings" page) at the entrance. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before the start time.

## Late arrival
Movies start with about 10 minutes of trailers. For concerts and theatre, latecomers may be seated only during a suitable break.

## Age limits
Some events have age ratings. Children under 3 do not need a ticket for movies if they sit on a parent's lap. Concerts and stand-up comedy are 16+ unless stated otherwise.

## Food and drinks
Outside food and drinks are generally not allowed. Every venue has a snack counter.

## Accessibility
Row A seats near the aisle (A5 and A6) are the easiest to reach for wheelchair users and guests with limited mobility. Contact support before your visit if you need assistance.
