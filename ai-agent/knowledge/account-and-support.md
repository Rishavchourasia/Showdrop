# Account and support

## Forgot password
Password reset is not available yet in this demo. Create a new account with a different email if needed.

## Where is my ticket?
After payment, your ticket is sent to the email address on your account within a minute. You can always see your bookings on the "My bookings" page.

## Contacting support
Email support@showdrop.example (a demo address). Include your booking number, for example "Booking #12".

## Payment failed
Payments are simulated in this demo and always succeed. If you see "Your hold has expired", the 5 minutes ran out: go back to the show and select your seats again.
