# Cancellations and refunds

## Cancelling before payment
While your booking is PENDING (during the 5-minute checkout window) you can cancel it at any time with "Cancel & release seats". Nothing is charged.

## Cancelling a confirmed booking
Confirmed bookings cannot be cancelled online yet. Contact support at least 24 hours before the show starts and we will cancel it and refund the full amount.
Within 24 hours of the show, tickets are non-refundable, except when the show is cancelled or rescheduled by the organiser.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

## Show cancelled or rescheduled by the organiser
If the organiser cancels a show you get a full automatic refund. If it is rescheduled, your ticket stays valid for the new date, or you can ask support for a full refund.

## Note
Showdrop is a demo application: payments are simulated, so no real money is ever charged or refunded.
